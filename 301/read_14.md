## [The Time When Women Stopped Coding](https://www.npr.org/sections/money/2014/10/21/357629765/when-women-stopped-coding)

Women have made significant contributions in the field of computer science since its early days. However in 1984, rather than growing, the number of women earning degrees in the field halted and began decining, even while they continued to prosper in other fields.  

Rather than one root cause, the reason can be atrributed to many potential influences. Around this time is when the home PC began be marketed, thus leading children to have early exposure to a computer.  

Marketing seemed to cater to boys, so by having more exposure and practice with using a computer; the experince level and advantage was evident by the time college classes rolled around, which also assumed students had a an understanding where some where starting from the beginning.  

### Answers.1  

1. What occurred during the same time as the beginning of the decline of women in computer science?
   - Home PCs began to roll out; marketing for tech catered to boys.
2. Why does it matter that males had been playing on computers growing up?
   - Expectations shufted from knowing nothing to already being familiar with material being taught by the time atudents enrolled in college.
